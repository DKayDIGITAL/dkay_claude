Month-by-month table

Model: every assumption except the setup cost is set per quarter (3 months); months after quarter 20 reuse quarter 20. Each month, new listings = organic listings + paid listings, where paid listings = marketing budget ÷ cost per paid listing. A share of each month’s new listings converts to paying after that quarter’s free period; paying listings shrink by the monthly churn rate (sold, rented or cancelled). Earning = paying listings × monthly subscription + registration fee on every new listing. Expenditure = fixed cost + marketing budget + one scout visit per new listing + gateway fee on earning. GST is not modelled. All figures are planning assumptions, not measured data.
