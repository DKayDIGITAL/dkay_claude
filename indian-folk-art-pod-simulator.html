Month-by-month table

Model: every assumption except the setup cost is set per quarter (3 months); months after quarter 20 reuse quarter 20. All amounts are in rupees. Each month, new buyers = organic buyers + paid buyers, where paid buyers = marketing budget ÷ cost per paid order. Every buyer joins a past-buyer pool that shrinks by 4% a month; repeat orders spread each buyer’s lifetime repeat orders over that pool. Buyers pay in US dollars, so the order value and the Printify/Printful cost (both entered in rupees at ₹95.7 per US$) move with the ₹/US$ rate input. Earning = orders × order value. Expenditure = fixed cost + art commissions + marketing budget + production and shipping per order + platform, payment, currency and GST-on-fee charges, refunds and artist royalty as shares of earning. Sales to buyers abroad of goods printed and shipped abroad are outside Indian GST (Schedule III); US sales tax and EU/UK VAT are collected by Etsy on top of the price. Income tax and seasonality (the Q4 gift peak) are not modelled. All figures are planning assumptions, not measured data.
